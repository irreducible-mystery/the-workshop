## Ledger discipline

- A row is **born** with a source pointer or it is a class-D proposal.
- Rows are never deleted; status changes are appended history (git carries the
  when and who).
- When a question is chartered, its row gains a link to `qN-<name>/` and the
  charter names its adjudicators before its benches.
- This file is inventory prose. Its adjudicator is its reader, one source
  pointer away — keep the pointers honest.
