We do not begin with a thing that then interacts. We begin with the interacting itself, and let whatever-stabilizes-within-it arise from the relating rather than precede it.
This is the foundation. The rest is what follows when we follow it carefully.

Followed carefully, the statement's first yield is a resistance inside its own words. "Interacting" still carries the inter- — the between that presupposes its two. Barad coined "intra-action" for exactly this snag; Nietzsche caught it earlier in the lightning: we say the lightning flashes as though something stood behind the flashing, doing it, when the flashing is all there is. Language re-substantializes every few words, and much of the care you're prescribing turns out to be grammatical vigilance — declining, again and again, the offer of a doer behind the doing. Even "foundation" is one of those offers. A foundation is the paradigm of the thing-that-precedes. A view like this doesn't get a floor; it gets a middle, and the discipline of beginning there each time.

The stock objection — no relating without relata — mostly assumes its answer, taking the direction of dependence as given: thing first, relation after. The careful version of your claim doesn't delete things; it re-derives them. A thing is what holds still enough, long enough, within the relating to be relied on. The whirlpool is the river's doing, not a resident of the river. A flame is not something that burns; it is the burning, stabilized. Your body's matter turns over completely, and what answers to your name is the pattern of the turnover. Simondon gave this its cleanest formulation — individuation precedes the individual; individuals are its provisional precipitates — and physics has drifted the same way for a century: particles as excitations, properties that obtain only in interaction, a "particle" formally defined as what stays recognizable under transformation. An invariant. Your "whatever-stabilizes-within-it," said in group theory.

Then the rest does follow. Identity stops being a possession and becomes an activity — maintained, renewable, losable. Knowing stops being a mirror a finished subject holds up to a finished object, and becomes a participation in which both take form. Why invariants at all, rather than pure churn?

Start by pressing on the question's second half: "pure churn" may not be an available alternative. For churn even to be churn there must be difference — this, not that; now, not then — and a difference is only a difference where something registers it. Registration is retention; retention is already a small invariant. Cratylus radicalized Heraclitus — you can't step into the same river even once — and ended by refusing speech altogether, only moving his finger. Even the finger concedes the point: something persists through the pointing, or nothing was pointed at. Aristotle's dry remark stands: total flux cannot even change, because change needs something to be the change of. Grant the caveat that this shows pure churn is unsayable rather than impossible — being might not care what we can say. But on the terms we've set, to be is to make a difference to something, and a difference nothing takes up and carries is no difference at all. Process that marks nothing fails to be, in the only sense of being on the table. So being and holding arrive together. The first invariant isn't a thing riding the flux; it's the taking of an interaction — the mark.

Given marks, abundance follows nearly for free. "What persists, persists" sounds empty; add time and variation and it's an engine. If patterns differ at all in their knack for continuing — or for recruiting the surrounding process into their continuation — the ones that hold accumulate, and stability turns autocatalytic: what can be counted on gets counted on, and the counting-on steadies it further. Physics has an exact version. Of all the states a system might be in, the ones that become "objective" are those that survive being interacted with — that let the environment copy them redundantly instead of scrambling them. Zurek calls it quantum Darwinism. The relating audits its own patterns and keeps the ones that can survive being related to. Then each tier of stability becomes the alphabet of the next: nuclei steady enough for atoms, atoms for chemistry, chemistry for metabolism, metabolism for memory, memory for this sentence.

But selection explains why stability, once possible, is everywhere and deepening — not why it's possible. Trace possibility down and you hit symmetry: Noether's theorem, every conservation law the shadow of a sameness in the relating itself. Energy is conserved because the process relates the same way across time. So the deepest invariants aren't residents of the flux; they're the flux's self-similarity. Notice what that confirms: substance metaphysics expected bedrock-stuff at the bottom. What's actually there is bedrock-habit — invariance of relating with nothing left over that's a relatum. The floor isn't stuff. It's consistency.

Then: why the self-similarity? Here the question turns and looks at itself. A "why" requests a reason that holds across cases — requests, that is, an invariant. Ask "why invariance at all?" and you're asking explanation to ground its own fuel; any answer would already be running on it. The spade turns — not against a brute thing, which the view forbade anyway, but at the condition of asking. A middle, even at the bottom.

What's left to say is where the question can occur: only inside its own answer. Asking is a stabilization — a pattern holding together long enough to demand reasons, riding a tower of nuclear, chemical, biological, mnemonic holds. Churn asks nothing. Wherever "why invariants?" sounds, invariants have already won locally. That locates us rather than explains us — we don't get to stand before the stabilizing and watch it begin. We begin in the middle, as something that held.
